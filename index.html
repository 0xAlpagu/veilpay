ENCRYPTION
Salary amounts, encrypted before they ever touch the chain.
Every payment is encrypted client-side with AES-256-GCM before it's sent. The contract only ever stores ciphertext — never a plaintext number.
👁️
SELECTIVE DISCLOSURE
Only the people you choose can see the number.
Employees decrypt their own salary. Auditors or HR can be granted access explicitly — nobody else, including other employees, can read it.
💵
REAL SETTLEMENT
Funded and paid out in real USDC.
Payroll is funded on-chain in USDC and withdrawn directly to the employee's wallet. Encryption protects the amount right up until withdrawal.
🧭
ARC PRIVACY ROADMAP
Built to plug into Arc's upcoming privacy precompile.
Designed against the interface described in Circle's Arc Privacy Sector whitepaper, so it's forward-compatible once the real precompile ships on testnet.
